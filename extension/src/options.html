magic-manager companion

Reads what only your browser can see and, after you approve each time, hands the card lines to your magic-manager app. Version .

Pair with your app

Paste the exact address the app shows under Browser companion in its header. Only that address can talk to the companion.

What it may read

Mana Pool cart Your cart page on manapool.com.
What it reads

Cards, finishes, quantities and prices from your cart page. Never Mana Pool’s API, your sign-in or your address.

Always on
Allow access Everything else it can read, when the app asks: other carts, store tabs, Moxfield, Archidekt and order history. Chrome asks once for all of these sites. Some of these sites are allowed and some aren’t. Switch on to allow the rest.
What it covers
  • TCGplayer cart on www.tcgplayer.com: cards, conditions, quantities and prices. Never the sellers, your sign-in or your address.
  • Card Kingdom cart on www.cardkingdom.com: cards, grades, quantities and prices. Never your sign-in or your address.
  • Deals on the Deals stores: which of their tabs are open, and the price on product pages you have open.
  • Moxfield on moxfield.com: a deck, your collection or a binder you choose. Shared links need no sign-in. For your own private ones it uses Moxfield’s sign-in the way moxfield.com itself does: a short-lived token, kept in memory for that one read and sent only to Moxfield. Never stored, never sent to the app.
  • Archidekt on archidekt.com: a deck you choose, including your own private decks when you’re signed in. Only its cards, never your sign-in.
  • Order history on store.tcgplayer.com and cardkingdom.com: orders, items and prices paid. Never your address, payment or tracking. Mana Pool orders need nothing extra.

Switching it off removes all of these sites at once. Chrome’s own site-access page still lists each site separately.

What it never does

  • Read cookies, passwords, email or addresses.
  • Send a sign-in token anywhere but its own site. Moxfield’s short-lived token, for your private Moxfield cards, is the only one it ever holds: in memory, for that one read.
  • Change anything on Mana Pool, TCGplayer, Card Kingdom, Moxfield, Archidekt or a store: no cart edits, purchases or form submissions.
  • Read anything unless the app asks and you press Send in its own window.
  • Collect in the background, run analytics, or load code or fonts from the internet.

The source is plain JavaScript in the magic-manager repo, under extension/. Read it before you install.
