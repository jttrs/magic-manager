magic-manager companion

Reads things only your own browser can see and, after you approve each time, hands the card lines to your magic-manager app. Version .

1 · Pair with your app

The address you open magic-manager at. The companion answers only that address — no other website can talk to it.

2 · Choose what it may read

Mana Pool cart — always on
Reads your cart page on manapool.com (cards, finishes, quantities, prices). Never its API, your sign-in or your address.
Deals — your open store tabs
Lets the companion see which tabs are open on the Deals stores and read the price on product pages you have open. Chrome will ask to allow those sites.
Moxfield decks
Reads a deck you choose from moxfield.com, as your browser sees it (including your own private decks when you're signed in). Chrome will ask to allow moxfield.com.

What it never does

  • Read cookies, passwords, sign-in tokens, email or addresses.
  • Change anything on Mana Pool, Moxfield or a store — no cart edits, no purchases.
  • Read anything unless the app asks and you press Send in its own window.
  • Collect in the background, run analytics, or load code from the internet.

The source is plain JavaScript in the magic-manager repo (extension/) — read it before you install.
