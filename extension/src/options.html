magic-manager companion

Reads what only your browser can see and, after you approve each time, hands the card lines to your magic-manager app. Version .

Pair with your app

Paste the exact address the app shows under Browser companion in its header. Only that address can talk to the companion.

What it may read

Mana Pool cart Your cart page on manapool.com.
What it reads

Cards, finishes, quantities and prices from your cart page. Never Mana Pool’s API, your sign-in or your address.

Always on
TCGplayer cart Your cart page on www.tcgplayer.com, when you check it in the app.
What it reads

Cards, conditions, quantities and prices. Never the sellers, your sign-in or your address. Chrome asks to allow www.tcgplayer.com.

Card Kingdom cart Your cart page on www.cardkingdom.com, when you check it in the app.
What it reads

Cards, grades, quantities and prices. Never your sign-in or your address. Chrome asks to allow www.cardkingdom.com.

Deals Your open tabs on the Deals stores.
What it reads

Which of those stores’ tabs are open, and the price on product pages you have open. Chrome asks to allow those sites.

Moxfield A deck, your collection or a binder you choose.
What it reads

Read from moxfield.com as your browser sees it. Shared links need no sign-in. For your own private ones it uses Moxfield’s sign-in the way moxfield.com itself does: a short-lived token, kept in memory for that one read and sent only to Moxfield. Never stored, never sent to the app. Chrome asks to allow moxfield.com.

Archidekt A deck you choose.
What it reads

Read from archidekt.com as your browser sees it, including your own private decks when you’re signed in. Only its cards, never your sign-in. Chrome asks to allow archidekt.com.

Order history Your TCGplayer and Card Kingdom order pages, when the app asks.
What it reads

Orders, items and prices paid from store.tcgplayer.com and cardkingdom.com. Never your address, payment or tracking. Mana Pool orders need nothing extra. Chrome asks to allow those two sites.

What it never does

  • Read cookies, passwords, email or addresses.
  • Send a sign-in token anywhere but its own site. Moxfield’s short-lived token, for your private Moxfield cards, is the only one it ever holds: in memory, for that one read.
  • Change anything on Mana Pool, TCGplayer, Card Kingdom, Moxfield, Archidekt or a store: no cart edits, purchases or form submissions.
  • Read anything unless the app asks and you press Send in its own window.
  • Collect in the background, run analytics, or load code or fonts from the internet.

The source is plain JavaScript in the magic-manager repo, under extension/. Read it before you install.
