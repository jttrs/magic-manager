magic-manager companion

Reads things only your own browser can see and, after you approve each time, hands the card lines to your magic-manager app. Version .

1 · Pair with your app

The address you open magic-manager at. The companion answers only that address — no other website can talk to it.

2 · Choose what it may read

Mana Pool cart — always on
Reads your cart page on manapool.com (cards, finishes, quantities, prices). Never its API, your sign-in or your address.
TCGplayer cart
Reads your cart page on www.tcgplayer.com when you check it in the app (cards, conditions, quantities, prices — never the sellers, your sign-in or your address). Chrome will ask to allow www.tcgplayer.com.
Card Kingdom cart
Reads your cart page on www.cardkingdom.com when you check it in the app (cards, grades, quantities, prices — never your sign-in or your address). Chrome will ask to allow www.cardkingdom.com.
Deals — your open store tabs
Lets the companion see which tabs are open on the Deals stores and read the price on product pages you have open. Chrome will ask to allow those sites.
Moxfield decks, collection and binders
Reads a deck, your collection or a binder you choose from moxfield.com, as your browser sees it. Shared links are read without signing in. For your own private ones it uses Moxfield's sign-in the way moxfield.com itself does: a short-lived token, kept in memory for that one read and sent only to Moxfield — never stored or sent to the app. Chrome will ask to allow moxfield.com.
Archidekt decks
Reads a deck you choose from archidekt.com, as your browser sees it (including your own private decks when you're signed in) — only its cards, never your sign-in. Chrome will ask to allow archidekt.com.
Order history — TCGplayer and Card Kingdom
Reads your order history pages on store.tcgplayer.com and cardkingdom.com when the app asks (orders, items, prices paid — never your address, payment or tracking). Mana Pool orders need nothing extra. Chrome will ask to allow those two sites.

What it never does

  • Read cookies, passwords, email or addresses, or send any sign-in token anywhere but the site it belongs to (Moxfield's own short-lived token, for your private Moxfield cards, is the one token it ever holds — in memory, for that read).
  • Change anything on Mana Pool, TCGplayer, Card Kingdom, Moxfield, Archidekt or a store — no cart edits, no purchases, no form submissions.
  • Read anything unless the app asks and you press Send in its own window.
  • Collect in the background, run analytics, or load code from the internet.

The source is plain JavaScript in the magic-manager repo (extension/) — read it before you install.
