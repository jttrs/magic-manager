## 7. Edge cases & gotchas

- **Four Teferi, Master of Time printings** in the base frame (75, 275, 276,
  277) plus showcase 290–293 (same arts) and borderless 281 — eight `m21`
  Teferis. They're a functional quirk (phasing), not chase.
- **Showcase here = same art.** Unlike most sets' showcase (new art), M21's
  planeswalker-themed showcase only reframes. Don't assume showcase ⇒ distinct.
- Chromatic Orrery 228 / ext 382 and Terror of the Peaks 164 / ext 369 are the
  family's top cards; their ext versions are dropped.
