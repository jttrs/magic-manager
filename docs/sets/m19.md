## 7. Edge cases & gotchas

- **`pm19` is dated 2018-07-01**, two weeks before the set — the prerelease
  date, not the release.
- **`g18` released in November 2018** (holiday Gift Pack), months after M19,
  but is an M19 child.
- **Foil-only extras:** the five deck planeswalkers, Nexus of Fate and the
  Gift Pack cards have no nonfoil print.
- Five Planeswalker Decks (as in [`m20`](m20.md)); MTGJSON also
  lists five Welcome Decks, the Spellslinger Starter Kit and 15 Arena Starter
  Decks.
