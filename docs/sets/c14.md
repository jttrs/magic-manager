## 7. Edge cases & gotchas

- **No foil `c14` cards.** A foil Daretti from this product is the oversized
  `oc14` 33.
- **The first planeswalker commanders** ("can be your commander" on the card);
  a `Legendary Creature` type search misses them.
- **New-card band is 1–61** (62 Artisan of Kozilek is the first reprint).
