> **Five two-colour Commander decks with experience counters.** Standalone root,
> base frame. Unlike [`c16`](c16.md)–[`c19`](c19.md), **nothing in `c15` is
> foil** — the face commanders are nonfoil, and the only foils are the
> oversized `oc15` cards.

## 7. Edge cases & gotchas

- **No foil `c15` cards at all.** A "foil Kalemne" from this product is the
  oversized `oc15` 45 (memorabilia) — or a later printing (`cm2` 7).
- **New-card band is 1–56** (56 Command Beacon reprinted later; 57 Ajani's
  Chosen is the first reprint).
- **`tc15` 0 Experience** is a counter card, like `tcma`'s.
