## 7. Edge cases & gotchas

- **Foil only on the 20 legends.** Rows 21–322 are nonfoil only. The foil
  legends come from the decks (face commander + its alternate commander), not
  from boosters.
- **The Ikoria companion/mutate legends live here**, not in `iko`. A question
  about Kalamax, Otrimi or Gavi is a `c20` question.
- **The free-spell cycle** (Fierce Guardianship, Deflecting Swat, Deadly
  Rollick, Flawless Maneuver, Obscuring Haze) is the family's money. They were
  later reprinted elsewhere, so the `c20` print isn't the only source.
