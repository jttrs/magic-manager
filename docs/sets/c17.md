## 7. Edge cases & gotchas

- **The mythic legends are foil-only**, as in `c18`/`c19`. A nonfoil Edgar
  Markov 36 doesn't exist in this set.
- **The basics at 296–309 reuse the `c16` basics' art**, the same art as the
  `g17` Gift Pack and `pss2` Standard Showdown basics.
- **New-card band is 1–56** (56 Path of Ancestry was later reprinted; 57 Blind
  Obedience is the first reprint).
