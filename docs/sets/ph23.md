**Anchor code:** `ph23`
**Family root type:** `funny` (Heroes of the Realm — non-retail, not tournament-legal)
**Family released:** 2025-08-24 (Scryfall date for the 2023 edition; the series ships well after its nominal year)
**Last audit:** 2026-11-01 via `/characterize-set ph23` (non-interactive batch run).

**What this is:** a 2-card **special product**, not a retail set. Heroes of the
Realm cards are one-off black-bordered "funny" cards WotC hands out
outside retail (not sold in boosters or any SKU) — there is no product to buy,
no booster data, and no Scryfall/TCGplayer price on either card. It is one
yearly edition of a series; each year is its own separately-rooted Scryfall
set (see §1).

## 7. Edge cases & gotchas

- `set_type: funny` — not tournament-legal; Mr. Monopoly is a Hasbro (Monopoly) crossover joke card (−40: create 200 Treasure). Ormacar introduces a joke "Precious" partner-style keyword (a second commander that is a legendary noncreature artifact).
- Both cards: black border, `security_stamp: oval`, `layout: normal`, `full_art: false`.
- **No prices:** Scryfall carries no `usd`/`usd_foil` for either card, so value rollups and `set_status` show $0; `missing.is_concentrated` cannot fire.
- Scryfall `released_at` (2025-08-24) is ~2 years after the edition year — don't infer the year from the date. The series always lags (earlier editions are dated ~1 year after their edition year, §1); ph23's lag is the longest.
- Digital-only check: `games = [paper]` on both; nothing arena-stamped.
- Each yearly edition is its own root — `set_status.py ph22` etc. report separate families, not siblings of `ph23`.
