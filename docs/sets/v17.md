## 7. Edge cases & gotchas

- **Rarity is all mythic**, whatever the original printing was (Delver of
  Secrets is a mythic here).
- **`5b` Brisela** is the meld result printed on the back of Bruna's and
  Gisela's halves; it has no price and no separate purchase.
- **Foil-only:** there are no nonfoil prints.
