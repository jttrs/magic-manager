> **Jumpstart product, not a booster-fun expansion.** `j22` is sold only as

## 2. Treatments

Survey (`survey_treatment_signature.py J22`, 881 prints): 880 `regular`
nonfoil + 1 `ext` foil. No showcase, borderless, etched, or fancy-foil tier.

| Class | Count | CN band | Computed treatment | Preferred-filter disposition |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| base (regular, nonfoil-only) | 834 | `j22` 1–834 | `regular` | handled by rarity/base sub-selectors |
| front cards (memorabilia, nonfoil) | 46 | `fj22` 1–46 | `regular` | memorabilia — off master-list / Collection by default |
| extended-art (foil, release promo) | 1 | `j22` 835 (Kibo, Uktabi Prince) | `ext` | auto-dropped by `preferred` (ext class) |

`frame_effects` on base prints are cosmetic only: `legendary` 45, `snow` 11,
`enchantment` 8, `devoid` 7, `legendary+snow` 2; 807 none.

**Base CN bands** (all `regular`, so the treatment filter doesn't distinguish them):

- **1–51** — 51 cards new to paper in J22 (`not:reprint`), e.g. Preston, the
  Vanisher (8), Ashcoat of the Shadow Swarm (19), Zask, Skittering Swarmlord (47),
  Pirated Copy (16), Kibo, Uktabi Prince (40).
- **52–97** — 46 reprints with **new artwork** (Balan 53, Kiki-Jiki 79, Karn
  Liberated 97, …). Five of them also appear later with their old art:
  Valorous Stance 59/263, Stitcher's Supplier 73/472, Tragic Slip 74/478,
  Dragon Fodder 76/523, Thermo-Alchemist 83/613 (different `illustration_id`
  each — two distinct printings, both kept).
- **98–112** — basics, 3 arts each of Plains/Island/Swamp/Mountain/Forest.
- **113–834** — reprints (Rhystic Study 114, Teferi's Puzzle Box 801, Urza
  lands 830–831, Snow-Covered Island 833, Wastes 834, …).

## 7. Edge cases & gotchas

- **Release promo inside the main set:** `j22` 835 Kibo is the only foil and the
  only `promo_types` print; distinct art from main-set Kibo CN 40. Unpriced.
- **Same-name pairs at two CNs** (new art 52–97 vs old art 113+): Valorous
  Stance, Stitcher's Supplier, Tragic Slip, Dragon Fodder, Thermo-Alchemist.
  When adding by name, confirm which art the user holds.
- **`fj22` front cards** are memorabilia, unpriced, and used by
  `front_cards.py` as Jumpstart theme art (46 themes); not in checklists by
  default.
- No digital-only/arena-stamped prints leak (`mm query show 'set:j22+related missing' | grep -i arena` → 0).
  (J22 also existed on Arena but Scryfall keeps that separate; `j22` is paper.)
- No tokens, meld backs, or serialized prints.
