## 7. Edge cases & gotchas

- **Finish split:** 125 cards (a third of the set) are two printings, nonfoil
  `N` and foil `N★`. Always match CN + finish; a selector that counts distinct
  printings double-counts these cards.
- **No mythics**: rare-regular is the top sub-selector.
- Scryfall lists 510 cards; 508 are local.
- MTGJSON: 5 Theme Decks, 10 Sample Decks, 2 Bundle Land Packs, MTGO
  Redemption — none in `PRECON_MODERN_TYPES`.
