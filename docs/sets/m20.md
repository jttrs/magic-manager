## 7. Edge cases & gotchas

- **Foil-only extras:** the buy-a-box Rienne and the five deck planeswalkers
  exist only in foil; their deck-mates and the 302–344 band are nonfoil only.
- **`ppp1` is a separate code** for the stamped basics; a `stamped` rule
  scoped to `pm20` alone would leave them in.
- **The bundle promo is in `pm20`, not `m20`** (unlike [`thb`](thb.md) /
  [`eld`](eld.md), whose bundle cards sit in the main set).
- MTGJSON's `starterdeck` band backs several products: five Welcome Decks
  (W/U/B/R/G), the Spellslinger Starter Kit (red + white) and ten Arena Starter
  Decks.
