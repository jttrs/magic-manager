## 2. Treatments

Every print computes `regular`.

| Class | Count | CN band | Computed treatment | Preferred-filter disposition |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| base | 249 | `m14` 1–249 (121 C / 60 U / 53 R / 15 M) | `regular` | rarity/base sub-selectors — R+M ≈ $234 nonfoil / $598 foil (Darksteel Forge 206 ~$52, Archangel of Thune 5 ~$47 / $77, Silence 35 ~$11 / $33, Liliana of the Dark Realms 102, Rise of the Dark Realms 111) |

**`promo_types`:** every `pm14` print except the resale `A12` has its own art.
The prerelease (`185★`) and release (`48★`) promos also carry `datestamped`,
so `missing.drop_datestamped_with_sibling` drops them (name + treatment
match, not art). No Promo Pack, no fancy-foil sheet → proposed
`[dupe_foil] m14 = []` to enable `mm query missing-set m14`.
