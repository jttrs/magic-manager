## 2. Treatments

Survey (`survey_treatment_signature.py A25`): all `regular`, no `promo_types`.
`frame_effects` `enchantment` (4 prints) is cosmetic.

| Class | Count | CN band | Computed treatment | Preferred-filter disposition |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| base (foil + nonfoil) | 249 | `a25` 1–249 (101 C / 80 U / 53 R / 15 M) | `regular` | rarity/base sub-selectors — R+M ≈ $321 nonfoil / $816 foil |
| tokens | 16 | `ta25` | `regular` | tokens |

Top cards: Jace, the Mind Sculptor 62 (~$24), Animar, Soul of Elements 196
(~$22 / $84 foil), Pact of Negation 68 (~$18), Luminarch Ascension 23,
Chalice of the Void 222, Imperial Recruiter 136, Rishadan Port 246.

**New art:** 26 prints don't share an illustration with any other local
printing (Rishadan Port 246, Pendelhaven 244, Doomsday 88, Flash 57, Living
Wish 179, Squadron Hawk 34, Red Elemental Blast 147, …). The local catalog
undercounts older sets, so treat the count as an upper bound.

**`promo_types` dupe-foil / stamped signals:** none. Proposed
`[dupe_foil] a25 = []` to enable `mm query missing-set a25`.

## 7. Edge cases & gotchas

- **Rarities are re-assigned for the Masters set**; a card's rarity here can
  differ from its original printing.
- No basics; the draft booster has no land slot.
- Masters sets carry no Promo Pack, FNM or prerelease promos, so the whole
  family is booster-obtainable.
