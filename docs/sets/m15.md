> **Core set that introduced the modern (holofoil-stamp) card frame**, base
> frame only. Its **five prerelease promos have their own art** (unlike later
> sets' datestamped dupes) yet carry `datestamped`. Extras: a Sample Deck band
> (270–284), the Clash Pack `cp1`, and the M15 Prerelease Challenge `ppc1`.

## 7. Edge cases & gotchas

- **Prerelease promos here are unique art, and missing-set hides them.**
  `missing.drop_datestamped_with_sibling` keys on name + treatment, so each
  is dropped as a "dupe" of its booster card — the clearest test case for an
  `illustration_id`-aware version of that rule.
- **Prerelease packs are per-theme** (`prerelease-<ambition|ferocity|guile|strength|valor>`,
  EV ~$6.95–$9.01); MTGJSON also lists a `duelspromo` booster type.
