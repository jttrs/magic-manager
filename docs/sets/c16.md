## 7. Edge cases & gotchas

- **The legends are foil-only** — 20 of them, because each partner legend is
  its own card (`c17`/`c18` have 12 each).
- **New-card band is 1–56** (55 Prismatic Geoscope and 56 Ash Barrens were
  later reprinted; 57 Abzan Falconer is the first reprint).
- **The `c16` basics' art is reused** by [`c17`](c17.md), [`g17`](g17.md), the
  XLN Standard Showdown `pss2`, `cm2` and `cmm`.
