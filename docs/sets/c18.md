## 7. Edge cases & gotchas

- **The mythic legends/walkers are foil-only** (no nonfoil print), as in
  [`c19`](c19.md). A nonfoil Estrid 40 doesn't exist in this set.
- **Planeswalker commanders:** the four face commanders are planeswalkers
  that "can be your commander" — a type-line search for `Legendary Creature`
  misses them.
- **New-card band is 1–51** (all 51 have no earlier print).
