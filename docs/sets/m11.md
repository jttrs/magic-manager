## 2. Treatments

Every print computes `regular`.

| Class | Count | CN band | Computed treatment | Preferred-filter disposition |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| base | 249 | `m11` 1–249 (121 C / 60 U / 53 R / 15 M) | `regular` | rarity/base sub-selectors — R+M ≈ $193 nonfoil / $700 foil (Serra Ascendant 28 ~$30 / $62, Birds of Paradise 165, Platinum Angel 212, Temple Bell 217, Primeval Titan 192 ~$54 foil, Silence 30) |

**`promo_types`:** 3 of 4 `pm11` prints have their own art; the buy-a-box Birds of Paradise `165★` (~$44) is the priciest promo. The prerelease and release promos carry `datestamped`, so `missing.drop_datestamped_with_sibling` drops them from missing-set (name + treatment match, not art). No fancy-foil sheet →
configured `[dupe_foil] m11 = []` (2026-11-08) to enable `mm query missing-set m11`.
