## 7. Edge cases & gotchas

- **The mythic legends are foil-only** (no nonfoil print), unlike [`c20`](c20.md)
  whose legends come in both finishes. A nonfoil buy line for Kadena 45 would
  be wrong.
- **Three rare legends in the new band are nonfoil-only** (Gerrard 41,
  Grismold 44, Tahngarth 50) — they aren't deck commanders.
- **"New vs reprint" by local catalog undercounts reprints:** 15 reprints in
  66–302 (Prismatic Strands, Vesuvan Shapeshifter, Skinthinner, …) have no
  earlier print in the local `cards` table only because their original sets
  aren't synced. Use CN 1–65 as the new-card band.
