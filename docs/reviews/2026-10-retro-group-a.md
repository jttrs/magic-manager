3. **`/findings-review`** on the 77: an independent validator per P1/P2 finding (Opus for correctness, Sonnet for quality), P3s validated in per-PR batches. Validators could re-rate severity. **52 survive (0 P0, 0 P1, 17 P2, 35 P3); 25 refuted.**
